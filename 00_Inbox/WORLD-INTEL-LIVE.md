### Europe: The Pension Covenant Cracks

**[Panic and Anger Spread as Europe's Great Pension Covenant Unravels](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/panic-and-anger-spread-as-europes-great-pension-covenant-unravels-69a94ed7)** — *WSJ World News*
*Signal:* The postwar bargain — citizens surrender ~50% of pay for a state-funded retirement in their early 60s — is breaking under demographics, not ideology: France's "pension space" (fiscal headroom before taxation becomes self-defeating) is projected to hit zero by 2030. France, Italy and Spain's pay-as-you-go systems have far less flexibility than the hybrid models in Denmark, Sweden and the Netherlands, which pre-funded decades ago. Switching models now would require borrowing to pay two generations at once — the exact trap France is in.
*Read:* [Europe's broken bargain](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1445709-europe-s-broken-bargain) — EU working-age population projected to fall 36 million by 2050 while defense spending needs (3.5% of GDP) collide with pensions already at 12.2% of GDP.

↳ Together these suggest: the petrol-bomb attacks on French security forces during this weekend's third consecutive protest weekend are downstream of the same arithmetic — a government trying to cut a benefit voters were promised for life, with no reform path that doesn't require borrowing from a future that has fewer workers to repay it. This is the same fiscal-stress pattern (Fitch's France downgrade, Lagarde's early ECB exit) flagged in last week's briefing, now visibly reaching the street.

**Central bank tightening, in brief:** *Bloomberg Economics* reports the [Bank of England](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/the-bank-of-england-ponders-a-new-normal-for-interest-rates) is reconsidering whether current rates are enough to contain inflation (decision Thursday), [Australia](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-01/australia-set-for-fifth-rate-hike-as-inflation-fight-intensifies) is set for a fifth hike this year amid a deepening housing slump, [Taiwan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/taiwan-said-to-ease-exporter-fx-rule-after-record-stocks-exodus) eased FX rules to stem record stock outflows, and [Poland's central bank](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/how-poland-s-central-bank-is-caught-in-political-standoff-with-pm-donald-tusk) is caught in a political standoff with PM Tusk — a synchronized tightening cycle running into political resistance almost everywhere at once.
