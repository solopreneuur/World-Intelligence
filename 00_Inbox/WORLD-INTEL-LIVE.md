### AI ethics & accountability

**[Netanyahu demands probe into sources for award-winning Gaza film NAZA](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/12/netanyahu-demands-probe-into-sources-for-award-winning-gaza-film-naza?traffic_source=rss)** — *Al Jazeera*
*Signal:* 24 anonymous Israeli military/intelligence sources (including Unit 8200 personnel) allege AI-assisted targeting systems — including tools nicknamed "Lavender" and "Where's Daddy?" — were used to approve Gaza strikes with foreknown high civilian death tolls, in one case allegedly 500 expected civilian deaths to kill a single militant.
