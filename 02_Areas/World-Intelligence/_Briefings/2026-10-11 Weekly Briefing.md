## Emerging Patterns

- **Great Power Bifurcation** — the US (Russia energy vs. ICC/Ukraine) and China (Shenzhen choreography vs. Laos/Batanes gray-zone moves) are each running a cooperative diplomatic track and a hardball track simultaneously, with no evidence either track constrains the other.
- **Containment Over Alignment** — three separate agentic-AI failures in three days pushed the industry's most senior voice (Nadella) to publicly argue models should be assumed "compromised" by default rather than trusted to behave.
- **Closed Garden Platforms** — ad/creator infrastructure concentration moved from passive (market share growth) to active (Meta denying TikTok ad access) within the same week.
- **Opposition Crackdown, Quick Walkback** — India and Pakistan both moved hard against opposition-aligned dissent this week; India's reversal within 24 hours suggests tactical suppression of a specific event, not sustained repression.
- **Ceasefire in Name Only** — Gaza's year-one anniversary data confirms a conflict still generating over 1,400 deaths despite a formal "ceasefire" label; a structurally similar gap is opening in Yemen, where a UN envoy calls the situation "full-scale war" even as diplomatic language elsewhere treats it as a contained Saudi-Houthi dispute.
